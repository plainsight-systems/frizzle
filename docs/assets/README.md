# Project artwork

`frizzle-readme.png` is the initial README and project-site banner, generated
2026-10-27 with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. It is 2171 × 724
pixels with an opaque background. The original PNG is preserved without
resizing or retouching. It is referenced by the root README.

The illustration uses the midnight-navy, antique-gold and textured-paper
visual language of Charlotte, Cradle, Serenity and Dolittle. A small vintage
coach drives along a night road through a long row of glass panes, each a grid
of points of light; on one pane a single point glows amber beside a brass dial
that has just been turned, and the road ahead grows warmer. The metaphor is
Frizzle's: travel inside the model's layers, turn one value up by hand, and see
what changes downstream. It draws on Ms. Frizzle's idea without using
Scholastic's characters or bus.

## Generation prompt

The save path in the prompt was an absolute local path; it is shown here
relative to the repository.

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: wide README and project website banner for Frizzle, a hand-built AI demo that runs a language model in the browser and lets you reach inside one of its layers, change a single value, and watch what the model says change.
Create an original panoramic illustration, aspect ratio 3:1, ideally 2172 by 724 pixels.
Scene: a miniature vintage motor coach, small and rounded, cream and brass with warm glowing headlamps, drives along a narrow road at night that runs through a long receding sequence of tall translucent panes, like sheets of engraved glass. Each pane is a fine regular grid of tiny points of light, most of them dim, a few softly lit, so the panes read as layers of a vast quiet machine stretching into the distance. On one pane near the middle, a single grid point glows strongly in amber, and a small antique brass dial mounted beside it has clearly just been turned. Beyond that pane, the light along the road ahead shifts subtly warmer, as if the change is travelling forward with the coach. These are a gentle visual metaphor, not a technical diagram. Keep the coach, the panes of grid lights, and the one brightened point with its brass dial as the three clear focal elements; avoid excessive gadgets.
Style: richly detailed vintage wood engraving and hand-pulled screenprint, fine crosshatching, tactile ink grain, slightly imperfect edges, restrained storybook warmth and a sense of adventure. Wide cinematic composition with a narrow cream textured paper border all around. Deep midnight navy shadows, antique gold and amber light, cream paper, small touches of muted brass. Strong silhouettes legible at banner scale, curious and inviting rather than comic or futuristic. Generous dark breathing room, carefully balanced horizontal composition, the panes receding in clear perspective.
Output: save the final image as an unresized, unretouched PNG at docs/assets/frizzle-readme.png (create the docs/assets folder if it does not exist).
Constraints: original scene and an original vehicle design, not a yellow school bus and not any known franchise vehicle or character. No human figure, no actor likeness, no animals, no title, no logo, no watermark, no readable words or numbers, no robots, no brain icon, no neon cyberpunk, no computer interface or screens, no speech bubbles, no arrows or labels. Opaque background.
```
