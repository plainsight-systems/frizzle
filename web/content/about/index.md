Frizzle is a tech demo from Plainsight Systems, research and development
built to understand something by making it. It follows
[Charlotte](https://github.com/plainsight-systems/charlotte), which runs
language models in the browser, and
[Dolittle](https://github.com/plainsight-systems/dolittle), which looks
inside their layers.

## The name

Ms. Frizzle drives her class bus inside things to see how they work: a
beehive, a bloodstream, the solar system. “Take chances, make mistakes, get
messy!” That is the idea here: travel inside a running language model, turn
something up by hand, and watch what it does to what the model says.

## The model

Gemma 3 1B, Google’s instruction-tuned model, in the 4-bit Q4_0 file
published by Unsloth on Hugging Face. Your browser downloads it from there,
checks it byte for byte against the file Frizzle is pinned to, and keeps it
on your device. Frizzle does not redistribute it. Gemma is provided under
and subject to the [Gemma Terms of Use](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/terms)
and the [Gemma Prohibited Use Policy](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/prohibited_use_policy).

## What it sends

Nothing. The page has no analytics and sets no cookies. The only request it
makes beyond this site is the model’s download from Hugging Face.
